Findings

  • Minimum fee follows compute mass, not storage mass. A 0.5 TKAS payment is rejected below 203,600 sompi even though storage mass is 20,000. The SDK's mass helper returns 20,000, so a naive fee overpays about 10×.
  • A zero-value output panics storage-mass math (divide by zero) instead of returning an error.
  • get_metrics fails on live 2.0.1 and 2.1.0 nodes: missing processMetrics.
  • The public resolver returns both rusty-kaspa 2.0.1 and 2.1.0, both claiming to be synced.
  • The tic-tac-toe runbook and its Cargo.toml name different vprogs branches. The game was not played: it needs a local demo node, and the seed does not go into a website.
